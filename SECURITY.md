# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **info@revaluar.com** with a description of the issue, the affected version or
commit and the steps to reproduce it. Please do not open a public issue for a
security problem.

We aim to acknowledge a report within 5 business days and will keep you updated
while we look into it.

## Scope

`subset.py` and its documented use. The tool reads font files and text you give it
and writes font files; it makes no network requests. Issues in its dependencies
(fontTools, uharfbuzz, brotli) belong with those projects, but we are glad to hear
about them.
